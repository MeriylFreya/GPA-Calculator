About GPA.io

Our Mission

We're committed to helping students worldwide understand and calculate their GPAs accurately and easily. Our mission is to provide free, reliable academic calculators that empower students to track their academic progress and make informed decisions about their education.

What We Do

GPA.io provides free, online tools for students to:

  • Calculate CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) across all semesters
  • Calculate SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average) with CIE and SEE marks
  • Convert marks to percentages and different GPA scales
  • Learn about grading systems used worldwide
  • Understand academic standards and best practices

Why We're Different

100% Free

No ads, no signups, no hidden charges. Our tools are completely free forever.

Privacy First

All calculations happen in your browser. We never store, collect, or share your academic data.

Highly Accurate

Built by educators with deep knowledge of academic standards and grading systems worldwide.

Customizable

Set up your own grading scale to match your institution's specific requirements.

Educational

Learn how GPA is calculated, understand different grading systems, and read helpful guides.

Our Commitment

  • Accuracy: We use verified academic formulas and test extensively
  • Simplicity: Easy-to-use interface that works on all devices
  • Transparency: Clear explanations of how calculations work
  • Accessibility: Free access for all students worldwide
  • Privacy: Your data is yours alone - we never track or store it

Who We're For

GPA.io is built for:

  • High school students tracking their academic progress
  • College and university students calculating GPAs
  • Students preparing for job interviews and applications
