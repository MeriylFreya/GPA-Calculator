Quick Questions Answered

Get quick answers to common GPA questions

What's the difference between CGPA and SGPA?
+

SGPA (Semester GPA) is your grade point average for a single semester, while CGPA (Cumulative GPA) is your overall GPA across all semesters. CGPA is what most employers look at.

Is this calculator accurate?
+

Yes! Our calculator is built to match standard academic formulas used by universities worldwide. You can customize the grading scale to match your specific institution.

Do you store my data?
+

No. All calculations happen in your browser. Your data is never sent to our servers or stored anywhere. Your privacy is guaranteed.

Can I customize the grading scale?
+

Absolutely! Our calculator lets you set up your own grading scale to match your university's specific grade points and mark ranges.

How is credit weighted in GPA calculation?
+

Credits represent the course weight. Subjects with higher credits have more impact on your GPA. The formula is: (Grade Points × Credit) ÷ Total Credits

What's the standard grading scale?
+

The most common scales are 4.0 (US), 10.0 (India), and percentage-based (0-100). Different universities use different scales, which our calculator supports.
