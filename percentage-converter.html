About Conversions

Percentage to CGPA (10.0 scale): CGPA = Percentage ÷ 10

Percentage to CGPA (4.0 scale): CGPA = Percentage ÷ 25

Note: Conversion methods may vary by institution. Always verify with your university for their specific conversion formula.

Quick Facts

  • Different countries use different grading scales
  • 10.0 scale is common in India
  • 4.0 scale is used in USA and many international institutions
  • Percentage-based systems are common worldwide
  • Always check your institution's official conversion method
