Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2024

1. Overview

GPA.io ("we" or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information through our website.

2. Information We Don't Collect

Data Calculations

All GPA calculations are performed entirely in your browser. Your academic data (marks, SGPA, CGPA, grades) is NOT sent to our servers. We have no way to access or store your calculation inputs.

Personal Data

We do not require login credentials or collect personal academic information to use our calculators. No data is stored or transmitted from our calculator pages.

3. Information We May Collect

Contact Form

If you contact us through our contact form, we collect the information you voluntarily provide:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Message content

This information is used only to respond to your inquiry.

Usage Analytics

We may use analytics tools to understand how our website is used, including frequency of visits and popular pages. This data is anonymous and does not identify individuals.

4. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to enhance user experience. These are typically harmless text files stored on your device. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, or advertising. These services may collect information as described in their privacy policies. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

6. Security

We take security seriously. Since we don't collect academic data, there's minimal risk of exposure. For any contact information you provide, we use standard security practices.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Know what information we have collected
  • Request deletion of your contact information
  • Opt out of non-essential tracking

8. Children's Privacy

Our service is designed for students of all ages. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent from parents/guardians.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Your continued use of our service indicates acceptance of updates.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us.
